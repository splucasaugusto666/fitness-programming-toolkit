# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors respectfully, regardless of their background or experience. Keep
criticism focused on code and documented behavior. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
attacks, doxxing and unwanted contact are not acceptable.

For private reports contact the future maintainer through a private GitHub security
advisory after publication. Until a maintained contact channel is configured, do not
post personal details in public issues. A maintainer may remove abusive contributions
or restrict participation while reviewing reports fairly.
